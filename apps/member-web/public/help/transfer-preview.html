Understanding Transfer Previews

A transfer preview checks an instruction and shows its expected result before execution. No funds move during a preview.

Common validation messages

Harbor may ask you to correct an amount, choose two different accounts, or review the available balance. These messages help you correct the instruction before continuing.

When verification needs review

A review means Harbor cannot continue that workflow yet. It does not mean a transfer occurred, and no funds have been moved.
